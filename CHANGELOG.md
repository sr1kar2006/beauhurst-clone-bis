# Changelog

All notable changes to the prototype. Prototype for client demo. All data shown is sample data.

## 0.3.0: CMA tender gaps (November 2026)
- Prototype labelling on every screen and export; deployment URL and sub-path from environment settings
- International coverage: about 12,000 companies across the UK, Ireland and 26 other countries, with country-aware registries, currencies and filings
- Data sources and methodology page, with source footers and provenance on every profile card
- Company comparison across countries, with currency conversion and period alignment
- Analysis grid with calculated columns, a formula engine, a report builder, and Excel, PDF and CSV exports everywhere
- Bulk exports as background jobs with an allowance check and a Downloads page
- Mock sign-in, Admin console with users, usage reports and audit log
- People across companies: person profiles, network graph and people search
- Ultimate and beneficial ownership, risk and financial search criteria, and up to 20 years of history
- News categories, monitoring rules, alerts bell and inbox
- Accessibility improvements, a help centre, support, and a service and governance overview

## 0.2.0: Company profile
- Fifteen-section company profile with sticky header, guided tour and Lumen Bank demo company

## 0.1.0: Advanced search
- Query builder, results, statistics, map, saved searches and the first guided tour
